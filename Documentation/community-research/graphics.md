# Graphics findings from the Anno Mod-Corner

Start with the [graphics toolchain](../ui-and-resources/graphics-toolchain.md) and [CFG and FC patches](../ui-and-resources/cfg-and-fc.md) for maintained procedures. This chapter gives Anno 1800 graphics case reports, their source context and leads for further checks. **Tool source** describes the inspected release or commit. **Vanilla data** describes the extracted example. **Community report** records a user's result, with **Pending** for an interpretation awaiting verification. Dates identify posts in UTC. Discord source links may require server access.

## Textures and material maps

The [historical texture reference](README.md#historical-texture-reference) summarizes Judekw's 2019 PDF and its distinction between Anno 1800 and Anno 2070 maps.

The June 2026 mipmap discussion used “mipmaps” for both DDS-internal mip levels and several numbered DDS files. Both exist in the same native Anno 1800 example. ### DDS Packer README and emitted channels

**Tool source, v1.4.0.** Anno DDS Packer groups image inputs by filename, packs or unpacks channels, and emits numbered DDS files using texconv. The tagged metal packing function duplicates the metal plane into RGB and puts ambient occlusion in alpha when present. The top-level README's output table instead describes separate metal, roughness and AO in R, G and B. The tagged normal packing function places gloss in alpha. The icon encoder requests `BC7_UNORM_SRGB`; its comment and the README call it DXGI 99, while Microsoft assigns 99 to `BC7_UNORM` and 100 to `BC7_UNORM_SRGB`. These checks compare source and API definitions. Actual conversion output from the release was not generated here. [Tool announcement](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1507277265309007954), [v1.4.0 update](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1514871193864245298), [README output table](https://github.com/annomodtutorials/anno-dds-packer/blob/v1.4.0/README.md#output-convention), [packing code](https://github.com/annomodtutorials/anno-dds-packer/blob/v1.4.0/packer/packer.py#L271-L344), [icon encoder](https://github.com/annomodtutorials/anno-dds-packer/blob/v1.4.0/packer/encoder.py#L100-L163), [Microsoft DXGI enum](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dxgiformat/ne-dxgiformat-dxgi_format).

**Vanilla data.** The brass smelter's night glow mask is DXT1/BC1, whereas the Packer's non-icon encoder generates BC7. A source-format mismatch warrants checking the actual target material. Runtime compatibility of the alternate compression and material channels is pending. [Non-icon encoder](https://github.com/annomodtutorials/anno-dds-packer/blob/v1.4.0/packer/encoder.py#L37-L94), [brass smelter headers](../ui-and-resources/graphics-toolchain.md#texture-lods-and-dds-mipmaps).

### Reskins, decals and lighting

- **Community report, 2026-06-09 to 2026-06-11.** An Arctic brass smelter reskin stopped appearing after its resource paths were edited. A responder posted a reduced version using renamed sidecars and a replacement diffuse texture while retaining native resources elsewhere. Native `heavy_09.cfg` references `.psd` names despite deployed DDS files in the extraction. The combination of edits means the report does not isolate a single cause. The supplied ZIP was inspected and contains a same-stem CFG, IFO and FC, five diffuse LOD files and a CFG reference to the native RDM. [Problem report](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1513972492501258260), [analysis](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1514535875906899978), [example attachment post](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1514537358790492220).
- **Community report, 2025-04-11.** The bakery tint discussion distinguishes diffuse color, two dye-mask inputs and CFG color factors. A separate answer suggests the green component of a glow map is used for daytime glow and discusses the contents of the bakery texture. The reported interpretation is scoped to this material and its exact mask layout remains pending. [Bakery tint discussion in #1800-code](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/578443576552325143/1360046491892322354), [follow-up](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/578443576552325143/1360053909753954514).
- **Pending, 2026-06-10.** A coastal decal that vanishes over water prompted a reminder to inspect the asset's separate `GroundDecalAsset` references as well as CFG decals. The thread compares both games and does not demonstrate a fix. [Coastal decal discussion](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1514118185446805574).
- **Official patch notes.** GU18.2 added shader and sound bank mod support and GU18.3 fixed a regression affecting moddable ambient settings. The May 2026 thread discusses atmosphere parameters and reports missing UI after particular graphics-budget edits. The thread does not establish safe VRAM presets, cross-game shader compatibility or an engine limit. [GU18.2](https://www.anno-union.com/updates/anno-1800-pc-game-update-18-2/), [GU18.3](https://www.anno-union.com/updates/anno-1800-pc-game-update-18-3/), [atmosphere discussion](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1506372913350054068), [reported UI issue](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1505386022605951120).

## Nested resources, water and particle examples

- **Community report and Vanilla data, 2026-08-12.** Editing and exporting a visible well mesh did not update the nested CFG that owned the RDM reference. In the extraction, `africa_ornaments_1x1_well_01.cfg` points to `data/dlc06/graphics/props/decoration/well_01.cfg` inside `Files/Config`. The latter points to `production_colony02_03_well_01_lod0.rdm`. A Blender screenshot shows this extra `FILE_well_01` layer. The discussion demonstrates the difference between re-exporting the mesh and changing the resource path in its owning CFG. The more invasive example moved the model out of that subfile and needs a check of the retained transforms and dependencies for other objects. [Problem and suggested direct subfile export](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1536976433828728863), [parent hierarchy](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1537158301538590872), [illustrated reparenting](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1537160009971015680).
- **Community report and Vanilla data, 2024-12-17.** The small steam shipyard still rendered water through its floor. The participants distinguished the visible cutout mesh from the IFO `WaterBlocker`, and the reporter attributed the failed cutout to overlapping vertices. The native `data/graphics/buildings/special/harbor_03/harbor_03.cfg` references `harbor_03_lod0_cutout.rdm` while the adjacent IFO separately declares `WaterBlocker`. The visual diagnosis remains a community result. Exact clipping and pathfinding rules still need runtime verification. [Shipyard discussion](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1318564578891927562), [reported resolution](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1318659408447930440).
- **Pending, 2023-06-17 and 2025-04-26.** A pre-GU18 report observed failures when sequenced particles had an FC without corresponding feedback sequences. In a later airship case a modder distinguished triggering a particle by `ParticleID` from making an already-running effect visible through a color transform, and read a plural `Conditions` value as a combination of individual conditions. The example `49152` equals `16384 + 32768`. Track timing, the lifetime of destruction effects, reserved conditions and the behavior on GU18.4 remain open. [GU16 observation](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1119653688965873694), [airship track timing](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1365752651941412885), [conditions and particle discussion](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1365752662707933295).


- **Community report, 2025-07-21.** A smaller warehouse displayed correctly after resizing its RDM but stayed red during placement. The response traced resizing through the CFG hierarchy, IFO and prop container. This is a lead for uniform scaling across different resource types. Check placement blockers separately from rendered dimensions, and follow the [IFO transform guidance](../ui-and-resources/graphics-toolchain.md#blender-scene-and-material-structure) for cuboids and planes. [Placement report](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1396725783258730568), [scale propagation](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1396730559258165258).
- **Pending, 2024-07-05.** A placement-light discussion suggested a `DAYNIGHT_TRANSFORM` in the light's CFG transformer list, with `TimeSlice=2` in the shared night-only example. A participant compared Blender previews with the native street lamp and observed a brightness difference. The numeric slice mapping and the difference between light textures and Blender previews remain unverified. [Transformer case](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1258894000799219723), [preview comparison](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1258905118175395874).
- **Community report, 2025-05-21 and 2025-08-13.** The game log showed missing resource paths when a custom embassy or spy hideout was loaded. One BFG error was reported even though the asset appeared to work. Missing-path reports help trace the packaged CFG and sidecars. The discussion also suspected long paths, but did not isolate a cutoff or prove the role of BFG. [Embassy resource log](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1374759486450958437), [later BFG report](https://discord.com/channels/578443209940926465/1080167315452203040/1405291446457864204).
